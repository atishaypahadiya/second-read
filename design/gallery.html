Contrast

WCAG ratios for text at o1 and o2 on every surface. The spec's light card (#8E→#B4) cannot carry white text at 4.5:1, so light cards use ink and white is kept for marks. The deep variant is where white text is wanted on a light sheet.

Glass sits over video, so its real contrast depends on the frame. At 62% of #1C1C1C over a mid-grey frame the effective background is about #4E4E4E, where white o2 still reads 4.6:1.
